Privacy Policy for Omnicalender

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy describes how the Android app Omnicalender (package name com.omnicalender) handles information about you. Omnicalender is a calendar and home-screen widget app showing name days, public holidays, religious feast days, theme days, moon phases, sunrise and sunset, and northern-lights forecasts.

The app is developed and run by Leine Johansson as a private individual.

Contact: leine@leinesblogg.com

Summary

  • You do not need an account to use the app.
  • The app has no servers of its own and does not itself collect personal data about you. Your settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Personalised ads are shown only if you have given your consent.
  • The optional purchase Omnicalender Pro is handled entirely by Google Play and removes the ads. The app never sees your payment details.
  • If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location for sun times and the northern-lights forecast.

1. Information the app handles itself

The app stores the following only on your phone:

  • Settings: chosen countries, calendars, religions, language, widget layout and colours.
  • Cached content: downloaded "On this day" texts and the latest northern-lights and cloud forecast, so the app works offline.
  • Last known position: your approximate location and the name of your town, if you have allowed location access.
  • Purchase: whether you have bought Omnicalender Pro, so the Pro features stay unlocked.

This information does not leave the phone, except as described in sections 2–5, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise GPS location.

2. Location

The app asks for permission to use your approximate (coarse) location. It is used to calculate sunrise and sunset, to show your town name, and to estimate the chance of seeing the northern or southern lights where you are.

  • The location permission is optional. Without it you can choose a city in the app instead.
  • When the northern-lights feature is on, your coordinates rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) are sent to MET Norway to get the cloud-cover forecast (see section 4).
  • To show the name of your town, the coordinates are passed to the phone's built-in geocoder, which is provided by the operating system (usually Google).
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android settings (Apps → Omnicalender → Permissions).

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location based on the IP address
  • information about the device, such as model, operating system and language
  • information about how ads are shown and whether you tap them

Consent: if you are in the EU/EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent form from Google is shown the first time you open the app. There you choose whether Google may show personalised ads. If you decline, only non-personalised (limited) ads are shown.

Changing your choice: you can withdraw or change your consent at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings (Apps → Omnicalender → Storage → Clear data); the consent form will then appear again. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings under Google → Ads.

Google is responsible for how this information is handled under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you buy Omnicalender Pro, no more ads are shown and AdMob is no longer called to display ads.

4. Content fetched from the internet

To show current information, the app fetches content from the following services. With each request the service sees, as usual, your IP address and that the request comes from the app.

Feature Fetched from What is sent
Cloud cover for the northern-lights forecast MET Norway (api.met.no) Your coordinates rounded to about 1 km, and the app's name
Aurora and geomagnetic forecast NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (swpc.noaa.gov) Nothing beyond the request itself; the same global forecast is downloaded by everyone
"On this day" in other languages (optional download) Wikimedia Foundation (Wikipedia) Nothing beyond the request itself and the chosen language

Name days, holidays, feast days, moon phases and sun times are calculated or stored in the app and do not require an internet connection. If you turn the northern-lights feature off, the requests to MET Norway and NOAA stop.

5. In-app purchase (Google Play)

Omnicalender Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. The payment is handled entirely by Google. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is complete, never card or payment details. The purchase is linked to your Google account and can be restored if you reinstall the app.

Read more in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information about children.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • know what information is held about you
  • have incorrect information corrected
  • have your information deleted
  • withdraw your consent

Since the app itself does not collect or store personal data outside your phone, you can exercise these rights by changing the app's permissions, clearing its data or uninstalling it. For information collected by Google, contact Google via https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY): https://www.imy.se
